| 2026-11-09 | WX-2.5 | One update every 30 minutes fetches both the 5 days and the 12 hours (2 calls, 96 a day). | 2026-11-09 | WX-6.7 | Later days open on their daytime and overnight halves, from the 5-day reply's Day and Night, not hourly forecasts. | 2026-11-09 | WX-6.8 | "precip" for the chance of precipitation (rain or snow), "cloud" for cloud cover, "humidity" for humidity; the top block spells them out (PRECIPITATION, HUMIDITY, CLOUD COVER). | Chris: "making clear which percent is cloud coverage vs precipitation". AccuWeather's figure covers snow too, so not "rain". |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.6 | The page shows the town's name only; settings still list "Hastings-on-Hudson, NY" so towns with the same name can be told apart. The tile's town (WX-3.4) is unchanged. | Chris: remove the ", …" by the town. The tile interface is fixed. |

| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.10 | Pull to refresh is the app's own: the page follows the finger at half speed (up to 140 dp) and refreshes past 72 dp, with a text hint. | androidx SwipeRefreshLayout draws a Material spinner (WX-6.1 forbids Material). |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.11 | Festive snow: evergreen-to-midnight stops, amber glow (#F2A23C at 28%) from the bottom-right, 34 soft white dots at fixed places.
