# Decisions

| Date | Req | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-08 | WX-2.2 | The key goes in an `Authorization: Bearer` header only, to `dataservice.accuweather.com`. | AccuWeather's Authentication page (developer.accuweather.com/documentation/authentication, read 8 Nov 2026): "Every request to the API must include a valid API key in the Authorization header", bearer format. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-2.6 | No current-conditions call. The page's top block shows today's high, low and figures from the daily forecast; the hours come from the 12-hour forecast. | One call fewer per opening; the 12-hour forecast starts within the hour. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-2.7 | The app stops at 100 calls a day (WeatherTokens). | Q1 (Chris's plan) was open. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-2.7 | The app stops at 450 calls a day. | Q1 (Chris, 9 Nov): the paid plan allows 500 a day; 50 spare for refreshes from a second install or a test session. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-2.5 | A failed daily update isn't retried until the next slot or a manual refresh, including when the app restarts. | The launcher starts the app's process each time it reads the tile; retrying on each start could spend calls with a bad key. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-2.4 | The default town's location key is found with one city search ("Hastings-on-Hudson", matched to NY, US) on the first update, then kept. | No key was available to look it up in advance. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-6.2 | Rounding ties go up: 72.5 → 75, 4 → 5, 8 → 9, −2 → −1. | The launcher's roundTo5 rounds halves up. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.2 | 0 is a target alongside the odd numbers (Chris: "0 should equal 0"): 0 → 0, 0.4 → 0, 0.5 → 1, −0.5 → 0, 2 → 3. | Nearest of {odd numbers, 0}, ties up, as above. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-6.2 | Settings' diagnostics (calls today, the key's last 4 characters, version, dates and times) are not rounded. | They aren't weather values, and "calls today" has to be exact to be useful against the plan's limit. Clock times aren't values either. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-6.3 | Hours get one of the five icons by the tile's thresholds, with cloud cover standing in for sunshine (sunshine share = 1 − cloud cover); rain or snow from a 50% chance when AccuWeather expects precipitation that hour. AccuWeather's icon numbers aren't used. | Same icons and rules as the tile (WX-3.3). 9 Nov: at night, the moon and partly cloudy night icons (Chris). |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-6.1 | Classic Views (WX-1.1), not Compose like the Tennis app. The top block uses the tile's gradient for today's condition (Palette.kt, copied from the launcher). | WX-1.1 is a MUST. The gradient ties the page to the tile without copying the tile. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-2.4 | Choosing another town clears the old town's forecast until the new one arrives. | Showing the old town's days under the new town's name would be wrong. |
| 2026-11-08 | WX-4.5 | Release key: EC P-256 (alias `weather`), made in the session; Chris pastes it into GitHub secrets. | Same as Wotworth/Tennis: short enough to paste from a tablet. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-2.9 | Three real replies (city search, 5 days, 12 hours for Hastings-on-Hudson, key 344606) recorded in the session with Chris's key, saved as test fixtures. They contain no key. CI still never calls AccuWeather. | Checks the field names against a real reply (WX-3.2). The key is held by the session's network proxy, never seen or stored by Claude. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-2.5 | One update every 30 minutes fetches both the 5 days and the 12 hours (2 calls, 96 a day). An update is skipped within 20 minutes of the last try unless Chris taps refresh. v0.1.4's four-slot work is cancelled on first start. | Chris asked for at least every 30 minutes; 96 + refreshes stays under the app's 450 cap. The 20-minute skip stops opening the app from spending calls just after a background update, and stops a bad key from spending calls. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.7 | Later days open on their daytime and overnight halves, from the 5-day reply's Day and Night, not hourly forecasts. Each half's icon follows the hour rules (WX-6.3). | Chris's plan refuses the 24-, 72- and 120-hour endpoints (403, "core-weather/starter"; 3 calls on 9 Nov to check). Q5 asks whether to upgrade. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.8 | "precip" for the chance of precipitation (rain or snow), "cloud" for cloud cover, "humidity" for humidity; the top block spells them out (PRECIPITATION, HUMIDITY, CLOUD COVER). | Chris: "making clear which percent is cloud coverage vs precipitation". AccuWeather's figure covers snow too, so not "rain". |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.6 | The page shows the town's name only; settings still list "Hastings-on-Hudson, NY" so towns with the same name can be told apart. The tile's town (WX-3.4) is unchanged. | Chris: remove the ", …" by the town. The tile interface is fixed. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.9 | The refresh icon turns until the forecast page is told the tile changed (each update, good or failed, says so) or 30 s pass, and always finishes its turn. | An update without a network waits in WorkManager; the icon must not turn for ever. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.10 | Pull to refresh is the app's own: the page follows the finger at half speed (up to 140 dp) and refreshes past 72 dp, with a text hint. | androidx SwipeRefreshLayout draws a Material spinner (WX-6.1 forbids Material). |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.11 | Festive snow: evergreen-to-midnight stops, amber glow (#F2A23C at 28%) from the bottom-right, 34 soft white dots at fixed places. App screens only. | Chris asked for slight, cozy and sophisticated; the tile's look is the launcher's (WX-5.1). Fixed dots, so screenshots stay stable. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.11 | Snowflakes are Fluent's weather_snowflake_48_regular (thin lines), eleven placed by hand (Chris: "add a few more"; sizes 14–64 dp, turned, 11–14% white), kept off the numbers in the top block. PaletteTest checks white text over a snowflake in the glow, and 75% white text over a snowflake outside it. | Chris asked for snowflakes; the Fluent icon matches the app's other icons and avoids hand-drawn shapes. Hand placement reads calmer than random. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.11 | Festive snow shows all year. | Q6 (Chris, 9 Nov): "Use this setting the whole year". |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.12 | Seasons are meteorological months in the northern hemisphere, by each day's own date; a card's look is the tile's gradient pulled toward the season's colour, darkened as needed for contrast, plus a corner glow and motifs at the snowflakes' hand-placed spots. | Simple and predictable; the default town is in New York. One layout keeps every motif off the numbers. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.12 | Motifs at the right edge (the two corner spots) are 2.2 times as opaque and full size; the rest stay faint. PaletteTest checks text over every non-edge motif and every glow. | The first version's motifs were too faint to read as leaves or eggs; the corners never hold text. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.12 | Spring's eggs are a plain egg outline drawn for the app (one smooth path, pastel, no pattern). Leaves, clover, sparkles and drops are Fluent. | Fluent has no Easter egg; its food_egg is a fried egg. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.12 | Snow keeps the festive look in every season, not a seasonal one. | Chris, 9 Nov (Q6): festive snow "the whole year". |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.12 | Second pass (Chris: not differentiated enough): each season has its own sunny gradient (violet–rose, green, orange–russet, ice blue), not a tint of the tile's. Decorations are bold (35–75% opacity) and each card leaves out any that would sit behind text or the icon, measured from the laid-out text (HeroTest checks it, at standard and 130% text). Replaces the corner-only bold motifs. | Bold everywhere would hide numbers; measuring the real text works for every phrase length and text size. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.12 | The top block's lows and labels are 85% white (were the tile's 75%). | Brighter season colours keep 4.5:1 only with slightly brighter secondary text. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.13 | Corner scenes and decorated eggs are flat vector illustrations drawn for the app (tools/scene_art.py); the fall pile reuses Fluent leaf shapes. | Fluent has no pine, cedar, maple canopy, grass or Easter egg. Flat shapes match Metro. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.13 | Cards with a scene keep the phrase and the three figures 84 dp clear of the right edge; the figures get a 10 dp gap. | Otherwise CLOUD COVER sits in the scene's corner. |
| 2026-11-09 | WX-6.13 | Decorated eggs are drawn to a picture once and turned as a picture. | Their bands are clipped to the egg shape; a picture draws the same at any angle on every phone. |
